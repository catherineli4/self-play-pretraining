Plots

overview

Cross-experiment summaries.

1m_replication

Self-play replication at 1m (`sp_1m_seed0`, 8192 rounds, 43B synthetic tokens, 5.2M per round) and the uniform-prior program baseline.

1m_prepretraining_plateau

Paper Appendix A protocol at 1m: train on the 270 MB DCLM stream until the validation bpb plateaus, from scratch over initialisation settings x LR x WD, and warm-started from self-play checkpoints (`results/1m_prepretraining_plateau`).

1m_prepretraining_fresh

Fresh-token protocol at 1m: one pass over the first 1B or 2B bytes of a 2B-byte DCLM stream (constant LR, 200-step cosine decay at the end), best of LR {3e-3, 1e-2} x WD {0, 0.1}, validated on a disjoint stream (`results/1m_prepretraining_fresh`).

25m_study

25m study: the self-play run `sp_25m_seed0` (6560 rounds, 34.4B synthetic tokens, 5.2M per round), the k-Dyck, NCA and DeclRef curricula of Rethinking Pre-pretraining (2B synthetic tokens each), the fresh-DCLM downstream comparison (one pass over 1B or 2B tokens, best of LR {1e-3, 3e-3}, WD 0.1) and the compute-matched scratch baselines (`results/25m`).

1m_reward_variant

Ablation at 1m: the learning-progress reward without the AdamW step operator (`--tangent_precond identity`, run `sp_1m_rawdelta_seed0`, 4096 rounds) against the baseline at matching rounds (`results/1m_reward_variant`).

25m_reward_variant

Ablation at 25m: the reward without the AdamW step operator for the full 6560 rounds (`sp_25m_rawdelta_seed0`) against `sp_25m_seed0` (`results/25m/reward_variant.md`).

1m_generator_variant

Ablation at 1m: the generator updated on its own fresh samples only (`--gen_update fresh`, run `sp_1m_genfresh_seed0`, 4096 rounds), against the baseline and the reward ablation at matching rounds (`results/1m_generator_variant`).
